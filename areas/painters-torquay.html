Painters you can trust in Torquay, 3228.

Torquay is the start of the Surf Coast — Front Beach, Jan Juc, Bells Beach — and the housing reflects it. Original 1960s-80s beach shacks behind The Esplanade, modern architectural coastal builds along the cliff streets, holiday homes through the back streets, and the more recent estates pushing back toward the highway. We've painted across all of it, working with the one constant that defines Torquay painting: salt air.

Coastal painting isn't suburban painting with sand. Salt air is corrosive — it attacks metal trim, breaks down lesser paint systems, degrades timber substrates, and shows up the gaps in inadequate prep within months rather than years. We use marine-grade primer systems, salt-tolerant Dulux Weathershield top-coats, careful timber sealing, and the prep that gives a coastal exterior a genuine chance at lasting 8-10 years instead of failing in three.

05 Category five

Surface Prep & Pressure Cleaning in Torquay

Pressure washing Torquay homes is non-negotiable before any coastal repaint. Salt air finds every gap, every missed primer, every short-cut prep step — and the failure shows up within a year. Pressure cleaning to remove salt and lichen, careful sanding, salt-tolerant primer systems, full crack filling — handled by the same crew.

Specialised prep work

Pressure washing

High-pressure cleaning of timber weatherboards, fibre cement, brick, render, exposed metal, decks, paths and driveways. Removes salt residue, lichen, oxidised paint and biological growth — critical step on coastal exteriors.

Surface cleaning

Detail cleaning of substrates pressure-washing can't reach — interior plaster, cornices, mouldings, salt-affected metal hardware, feature timber. Sugar-soap and TSP washes for salt removal.

Sanding and priming

Hand-sanding, mechanical sanding and full marine-grade primer systems matched to coastal substrate — salt-tolerant timber primers, marine masonry sealers, fibre cement primers, exposed-metal primers.

Crack and hole filling

Filling cracks, gaps, splits and salt-damage in timber, plaster, render and fibre cement — flexible exterior caulks, two-pack fillers and substrate-matched patch.

Minor repairs and patching

Replacing salt-damaged timber sections, repairing rotten weatherboard or fascia ends, patching render and fibre cement, re-securing loose trims. Coastal carpentry handled in-house so the paint job's foundation is sound.

Why painting Torquay is different

Three things Torquay homes teach us every job.

01

Salt-air exposure is the differentiator

Torquay is a coastal-painting environment. Salt air corrodes metal, breaks down lesser paint systems, accelerates timber weathering and finds every gap in inadequate prep. We use marine-grade primer systems and salt-tolerant Weathershield top-coats as default, not as upgrades.

02

Mixed coastal housing stock

Torquay ranges from original 1960s-80s beach shacks through to architectural coastal homes — fibre cement, weatherboard, render, brick, exposed metal trim. We size the system to the substrate and the exposure: beachfront jobs get heavier marine systems than streets two blocks back.

03

Holiday-season scheduling

Torquay's busy season runs October through April. We schedule major exterior work in the cooler months when salt and humidity are easier to manage, and holiday-rental interiors between bookings with quick turnaround scheduling.
