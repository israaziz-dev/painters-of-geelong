05 Category five

Surface Prep & Pressure Cleaning in Waurn Ponds

Pressure washing Waurn Ponds homes is essential before any quality repaint. Even on newer estate homes, prep matters — render hairline cracks, settling movement, builder-grade finishes that haven't held up all need proper assessment before topcoat. Same crew handles prep through painting.

Specialised prep work

Pressure washing

High-pressure cleaning of brick facades, rendered walls, weatherboards, eaves, paths, decks and driveways. Removes lichen, oxidised paint, biological growth before priming.

Surface cleaning

Detail cleaning of substrates pressure-washing can't reach — interior plaster, cornices, mouldings, joinery, timber and metal hardware. Sugar-soap and TSP washes.

Sanding and priming

Hand-sanding, mechanical sanding and full primer systems matched to substrate — oil-based timber primers, masonry sealers, render bonding primers, stain blockers.

Crack and hole filling

Filling cracks, gaps, splits, hairline render cracks and damage in timber, plaster and render — flexible exterior caulks, two-pack fillers, render patch.

Minor repairs and patching

Replacing damaged timber sections, repairing rotten weatherboard ends, patching plaster and render, re-securing loose trims before painting. Saves a separate trade call-out.
