Painters you can trust in Newtown, 3220.

Newtown is one of Geelong's most distinctive suburbs — leafy Federation and Edwardian streetscapes, the boutique strip of Pakington Street, and the historic surrounds of Queens Park. Over the past 25 years we've repainted dozens of period weatherboard cottages, double-fronted Federation homes, and renovated workers' terraces between Aphrasia Street and Skene Street — work that demands a careful eye for original detailing and a respect for heritage-appropriate colour palettes.

Painting in Newtown isn't the same as painting in a new estate. Many homes have older lath-and-plaster interiors, original timber weatherboards, lead-paint substrates from earlier eras, and intricate eaves, fretwork and verandah detail. We specialise in the prep work these homes need: lead-paint encapsulation, hand-sanding, gap-filling, primer systems for aged timber, and finishes that hold up to the freeze-thaw cycles of a Geelong winter.

05 Category five

Surface Prep & Pressure Cleaning in Newtown

Pressure washing Newtown homes is where every quality repaint starts. Older substrates (lead-paint, weathered weatherboard, cracked render, aged brick) need methodical prep before a brush touches them. Pressure washing, encapsulation, sanding, primer matching, crack filling and minor carpentry — handled by the same crew that does the painting. The work that makes a 10-year warranty possible.

Specialised prep work

Pressure washing

High-pressure cleaning of weatherboards, eaves, rendered walls, brick facades, paths, decks and driveways. Removes lichen, oxidised paint, dust and biological growth before priming.

Surface cleaning

Detail cleaning of substrates pressure-washing can't reach — interior plaster, ornate cornices, picture rails, mouldings, original timber and metal hardware. Sugar-soap and TSP washes where needed.

Sanding and priming

Hand-sanding, mechanical sanding and full primer systems matched to substrate — oil-based timber primers, masonry sealers, stain blockers and bonding primers for difficult surfaces.

Crack and hole filling

Filling cracks, gaps, splits, nail holes and damage in timber, plaster and render — flexible exterior caulks, two-pack timber fillers, plaster patch and lime mortar where heritage-appropriate.

Minor repairs and patching

Replacing damaged timber sections, repairing rotten weatherboard ends, patching plaster and re-securing loose mouldings before they're painted. Saves the cost of a separate carpenter call-out.

Why painting Newtown is different

Three things Newtown homes teach us every job.

01

Heritage weatherboard stock

A large share of Newtown's homes are Federation, Edwardian or Victorian-era timber weatherboards with intricate detailing, original mouldings and sometimes lead-paint substrates. They need slower, more careful prep than modern brick veneers — and the right paint system to last 10+ years.

02

Coastal-temperate climate

Newtown sits inland from Corio Bay but still gets coastal humidity, UV-heavy summers and frost-prone winter mornings. We use Dulux Weathershield and equivalent systems engineered for these conditions, with primers chosen specifically for aged timber.

03

Local crew, 5 minutes away

Our base in central Geelong is around 5 minutes' drive from Newtown CBD — which means same-day site visits, free quotes, and faster turnaround if minor touch-ups are needed during your warranty period.
